# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers on macOS who dictate in Traditional Chinese, English, or a mix of both while working in editors, terminals, browsers, and communication apps. They want text at the cursor with minimal setup and interruption.

## Product Purpose

LaSay turns a press-and-hold shortcut into accurate, paste-ready text. It preserves technical vocabulary, supports private offline transcription, and offers configurable cloud transcription and text cleanup when users prefer higher quality or flexibility.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, capable, developer-native. The product should feel fast and trustworthy without drawing attention away from the user's current app.

## Anti-references

Avoid dashboard-like settings, marketing decoration, unexplained AI terminology, and controls that expose provider complexity before users need it. Do not replace familiar macOS controls with custom interaction patterns.

## Design Principles

- Keep the primary workflow to one shortcut and one result.
- Present a safe recommendation before advanced configuration.
- Explain choices by user outcome, not model taxonomy.
- Preserve explicit user choices and surface failures clearly.
- Follow native macOS behavior and visual conventions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native controls, complete VoiceOver labels, keyboard navigation, sufficient contrast, and localized Traditional Chinese and English copy. Do not rely on color alone to communicate state.
